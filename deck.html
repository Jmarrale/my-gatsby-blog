Operating Plan

How one expert scales: design once, deliver by playbook.

  • The design library — three tiered reference designs (Foothill, Estate, Legacy) adapted per plan set. Design hours per home fall every quarter.
  • The sub bench — two vetted, licensed subcontractors per trade: security, network/AV, electrical. Scorecards, retention on draws, our commissioning checklist.
  • Quality gate — Jon signs every design and every commissioning. That signature is the brand.
  • Team of five by year-end — project manager (Q1), operations admin (Q2), second PM (Q3), designer-drafter (Q4). No installation payroll, ever.
  • Cash discipline — 30% deposit, progress billing at rough-in and trim, subs paid after owner draws, distributor terms on equipment. The model funds its own growth.
  • Working capital — a ~$150K line covers timing gaps in Q1–Q2 before deposits stack.
