Process

01

Intake

You submit the project — community, lot, phase, scope. We respond within two business days with a consultation.

02

Design

Every system is designed personally by your consultant: drawings, device schedules, and specifications ready for bid.

03

Placement

We place the work with licensed, insured subcontractors we know — and hold them to the design, the schedule, and the budget.

04

Commissioning

Systems are tested, documented, and handed over — to you at closing, and to your buyer with a proper orientation.

Principle

We hold no dealer line and sell no hardware. Our only product is the design — which means the specification serves the home, and no one else.
